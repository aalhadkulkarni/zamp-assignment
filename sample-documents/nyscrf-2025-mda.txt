PAGE 21 — OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The New York State Common Retirement Fund holds the assets of the New
York State and Local Retirement System, comprising the Employees'
Retirement System and the Police and Fire Retirement System. The
Comptroller is the sole trustee of the Fund.

The Fund is reported as a single fiduciary fund. There are no separate
plan columns in the statements that follow, and the figures given below
are for the Fund as a whole.

Amounts in this discussion are stated in whole dollars.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 22 — FINANCIAL POSITION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Investments. Investments of the Fund, reported at fair value, were
267,401,338,552 at 31 March 2025. Public equity remained the largest
allocation. The Fund's private equity and real assets programmes
together accounted for a further quarter of the portfolio.

Receivables. Receivables totalled 3,118,224,901, comprising employer and
member contributions due, accrued investment income, and receivables for
investments sold but not yet settled at the reporting date.

Assets and liabilities. Total assets, before deferred outflows of
resources, were 274,905,117,663. Total liabilities, before deferred
inflows of resources, were 8,442,190,338, the greater part of which
relates to investment purchases payable.

Net position. Net position restricted for pension benefits stood at
266,462,927,325 at the close of the year.

PAGE 46 — ACTUARIAL AND FUNDING (EXCERPT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Fund is funded on an annual basis through employer contributions
determined by the actuary. The assumed rate of return applied in the
most recent valuation is 5.90 percent, reduced from 6.80 percent
following the 2024 experience study.

The discount rate used to measure the total pension liability is also
5.90 percent. The two assumptions coincide in the current valuation but
are established separately and serve different purposes.

The funded ratio was reported at 94.1 percent on an actuarial basis.
Salary growth is assumed at 4.40 percent and price inflation at 2.90
percent.
