PAGE 26 — MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS: FINANCIAL POSITION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The System administers a single defined benefit pension trust fund. All
amounts in this section are expressed in millions of dollars.

Assets. Total assets of the pension trust fund, before deferred outflows
of resources, were 209,884 at 31 August 2025.

Liabilities. Total liabilities, before deferred inflows of resources,
were 6,742. The larger part represents securities purchased but not
settled at year end.

Net position. Net position restricted for pension benefits was 203,142.

Receivables. Total receivables were 4,118, comprising contributions from
the State and from participating employers, together with accrued
investment income.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 27 — INVESTMENTS: DOMESTIC PORTFOLIO
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The System reports its investment holdings in two schedules. This page
covers the domestic portfolio; the international portfolio is presented
in the Investment Section, which is issued as a separate document.

Domestic investments, at fair value, totalled 118,447 at year end. This
comprises domestic public equity, domestic fixed income, and the
domestic component of the real assets programme.

No combined figure for the total investment portfolio is presented in
the Financial Section. Readers requiring the total should refer to both
schedules.
