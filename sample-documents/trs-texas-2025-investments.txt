PAGE 8 — INTERNATIONAL PORTFOLIO
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This section reports the international portfolio. The domestic portfolio
is presented in the Financial Section of the Annual Comprehensive
Financial Report and is not repeated here.

All amounts in this section are expressed in millions of dollars, on the
same basis as the Financial Section.

International investments, at fair value, stood at 82,916 at 31 August
2025. The allocation spans developed and emerging market equity,
non-dollar fixed income, and the international component of the real
assets and private markets programmes.

Currency exposure is partially hedged in accordance with the policy
adopted by the Board. The hedging programme is reported at page 44.

PAGE 31 — PERFORMANCE AND GOVERNANCE (EXCERPT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The total fund returned 9.8 percent for the year, against a policy
benchmark of 9.1 percent. Three-year and ten-year returns were 6.4
percent and 8.2 percent respectively, both ahead of benchmark.

The assumed rate of return used in the actuarial valuation is 7.00
percent. The discount rate applied in measuring the total pension
liability is likewise 7.00 percent; the two are set independently.

Investment expenses, including external management fees, are disclosed
in the Schedule of Investment Expenses and are not reproduced here.
