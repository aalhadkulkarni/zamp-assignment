PAGE 17 — BASIS OF PRESENTATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Florida Retirement System Pension Plan is a cost-sharing,
multiple-employer defined benefit plan administered by the State Board
of Administration. The Investment Plan, a defined contribution
alternative, is reported separately and is not included in the amounts
below.

Readers should note that the tables and the discussion in this section
express all dollar amounts in millions. The financial statements
themselves are presented in whole dollars, and the two should not be
compared line for line without adjustment.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
PAGE 18 — FINANCIAL POSITION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Investments. The Pension Plan held investments at fair value of 187,443
at year end, against 176,918 a year earlier.

Receivables. Amounts receivable at the reporting date totalled 2,091.
This figure includes contributions due from participating employers and
income accrued on investments.

Assets. Total assets, before deferred outflows of resources, were
191,884.

Liabilities. Total liabilities, before deferred inflows of resources,
were 5,336.

Net position. Net position restricted for pensions was 186,548 at 30
June 2025.

PAGE 39 — INVESTMENT POLICY (EXCERPT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Board adopts an investment policy statement setting target
allocations and permitted ranges. The long-term expected rate of return
assumption is 6.70 percent, net of investment expenses.

The discount rate used in the actuarial valuation is 6.70 percent.

Asset class targets are reviewed annually. External managers are used
where internal capability is not maintained, and manager fees are
reported in the Investment Section.
